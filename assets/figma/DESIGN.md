## Brand & Style

The design system champions Indian heritage through contemporary digital craftsmanship. It bridges generational artisanal guilds with modern global commerce, balancing authentic warmth with enterprise-level transaction clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Rooted & Authentic:** Grounded in earth tones, tactile materials, and cultural reverence without descending into kitsch.
- **Dignified & Professional:** Clean layouts, structured product hierarchies, and high accessibility standards instill buyer trust and artisan dignity.
- **Accessible & Welcoming:** Fluid bilingual layouts (Hindi and English), voice-assisted interaction patterns, and generous hit zones accommodate diverse literacy and technical proficiencies.

### Visual Style
The visual execution fuses modern editorial minimalism with subtle artisanal tactility. It uses soft organic radii, warm cream canvas backgrounds, deep earthen jewel tones, and deliberate surface separation rather than clinical neutral grays or harsh synthetic shadows.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects natural craft mediums—indigo vats, terra-cotta clay, turmeric dyes, and aged parchment.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas (`#FFF8ED`):** Warm Cream serves as the universal page foundation, avoiding harsh display glare and lending an artisanal, handmade paper aesthetic.
- **Surface Clean (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white is reserved strictly for interactive product cards, modals, and input fields to guarantee true color representation for handcrafted goods.
- **Primary Deep Teal (`#176B6B`):** Represents credibility, heritage, and focus. Applied to primary CTAs, active navigation states, trust badges, and voice input anchors.
- **Secondary Terracotta (`#B86F52`):** Evokes raw clay and artisan heritage. Used for maker bio badges, craft story callouts, artisanal origin tags, and secondary action triggers.
- **Tertiary Saffron (`#E3A24B`):** Warm, luminous amber applied sparingly to promotional chips, festival collections, pricing discounts, and rating stars.
- **Typography & Neutral (`#30251F`):** Dark Espresso replaces absolute black across all textual and structural elements, softening optical contrast while preserving crisp legibility.

## Typography

The pairing between Noto Serif and Plus Jakarta Sans bridges literary heritage with functional utility.

### Type Roles & Pairing
- **Editorial Headings (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys the permanence, authenticity, and narrative depth of artisan traditions. Excellent natural pairing with Devanagari script for seamless English/Hindi bilingualism.
- **Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, humanist geometry ensures superior legibility in dense product descriptions, multi-step checkout flows, and micro-labels across low-resolution mobile devices.

### Bilingual Handling
- In dual-script presentations (Hindi alongside English), line heights are increased by 15% to accommodate Devanagari diacritics and shirorekha alignment without clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid responsive grid rooted in a 4px/8px incremental spatial rhythm, prioritizing high-touch ergonomics for mobile users while scaling into structured editorial galleries on desktop.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Margin: `1rem` (16px), Gutter: `1rem` (16px). Touch targets enforce a minimum of 48px height for accessibility.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid. Margin: `2rem` (32px), Gutter: `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid maxing out at a 1280px content container. Margin: `2.5rem` (40px), Gutter: `1.5rem` (24px).

### Layout Rules
- **Content Density:** E-commerce catalogs maintain breathing room between product cards using `space-lg` gaps to avoid a discount-warehouse feel.
- **Narrative Sections:** Maker story panels and heritage spotlight banners span full grid widths on mobile and reflow into asymmetrical 7/5 column pairings on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm ambient shadows and tinted structural layering rather than synthetic high-contrast drop shadows.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Tonal Tinting:** Shadows use Dark Espresso (`#30251F`) at low opacity instead of pure pitch black, preserving the natural warmth of the cream backdrop.
- **Resting Cards:** `0 2px 8px rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.04)` paired with an ultra-thin hairline border (`1px solid rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.08)`).
- **Hover & Active States:** `0 8px 24px rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.08)`, gently pulling cards forward to signify interactive affordance.
- **Modals & Overlays:** `0 16px 40px rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.14)` over a semi-transparent espresso wash (`rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

The geometry draws from handmade pottery, smooth river stones, and textile curves, avoiding sharp corporate corners.

### Shape Language
- Base elements (cards, text inputs, dialogs) standardise on `roundedness: 2` (8px base radius, 16px for large surface panels).
- Badges, craft pills, audio interaction buttons, and search chips utilize full pill contours (`9999px`) to invite physical touch and quick scanning.
- Media elements feature soft 8px inner radii matching card containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled with Deep Teal (`#176B6B`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, 8px radius. Height: 48px mobile, 44px desktop. Hover state darkens primary to `#115252`.
- **Artisan / Story Button:** Filled with Terracotta (`#B86F52`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Reserved for direct artisan contact and cultural background modals.
- **Voice Search Trigger:** Pill-shaped, Deep Teal outline (`1.5px solid #176B6B`) or subtle tint fill (`rgba(23, 107, 107, 0.08)`) with a microphone icon and bilingual indicator ("बोलकर खोजें / Speak to Search").

### Cards
- **Product Cards:** Surface is Clean White (`#FFFFFF`), separated from the Warm Cream background by an 8px radius, subtle 1px border (`rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.08)`), and soft ambient shadow. Product photography sits in a 1:1 or 4:5 ratio with seamless corners.
- **Artisan Profile Card:** Soft Terracotta tint header (`rgba(184, 111, 82, 0.08)`), geographic craft badge (e.g., "Kutch, Gujarat"), and audio voice snippet element.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage / Craft Badges:** Filled with Terracotta soft-tint (`rgba(184, 111, 82, 0.12)`), text in Terracotta (`#B86F52`), full pill radius.
- **Discount / Festive Tags:** Background Saffron (`#E3A24B`), text in Dark Espresso (`#30251F`), font weight bold, full pill radius.
- **Filter Chips:** Clean White background, Dark Espresso border, transitioning to Deep Teal with white text when selected.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Crisp White background, 1px border in Espresso Tint (`rgba(48, 37, 31, 0.2)`), rounding 8px, minimum height 48px. Active focus shifts border to 2px Deep Teal (`#176B6B`) without harsh glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Deep Teal fill when active with white glyphs. Touch area padded to 44x44px.

### Specialized Elements
- **Voice-Assisted Drawer:** Floating bottom-sheet on mobile with pulsating Deep Teal audio rings, live Hindi/English transcription preview, and clear tactile dismiss actions.
